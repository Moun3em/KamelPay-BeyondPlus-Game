# FACILITATOR SCRIPT — Five-Corner Compliance Simulation

**A phase-by-phase teleprompter for the MC and floor facilitators.**
The platform carries the mechanics and the teaching. Your job is the room: keep people moving, keep tables unblocked, and never reveal a card's answer — the platform explains every decision itself.

## Staffing model
- **1 MC** — owns the console + projection, advances phases, runs the Debrief. The only person who touches the console during the session.
- **Floor facilitators** — 1 per 2–3 tables (≈1 per 10–15 players). They walk the room, nudge idle tables, watch red flags, and act as the ASP for their cluster during Phase B.
- **10 tables / ~50 players → 1 MC + 3–4 floor.** · **5 tables / ~25 players → 1 MC + 2 floor.** · **3 tables / 15 → 1 MC + 1 floor.**
- One MC can run the whole game solo at ≤4 tables; use floor facilitators when tables outnumber the MC's line of sight.

## The golden rule for every facilitator
**You never know — and never need to know — whether a card is valid.** If a table asks "was that right?", point at the phone: *"Read the WHY modal — it shows the regulation behind your decision. That's the answer."* The platform explains every FILE/QUARANTINE outcome, correct or incorrect, on the player's phone. Your job is not the verdict — it's the room.

## T−30 min — Setup (MC only, console + laptop)
- [ ] Projection up: `/projection` · Console open: `/console` (PIN on the console screen)
- [ ] **Reset game** (reason: "pre-event reset") → phase LOBBY, every table 1,000,000
- [ ] **Set active tables** to the real headcount (3–10)
- [ ] Verify every table row shows before people arrive
- [ ] Hand each table its envelope: **table tent (PIN + join link + role list) · card deck · how-to-play card · reference sheets (Compliance Directive / Risk Register)**
- [ ] Brief the floor facilitators: the script, the red flags, the ASP ritual, the emergency table

## LOBBY — 09:00 (10 min) — "Get everyone in"
**Say:** *"Open the link on your phones, enter your table number and the PIN on your tent, and claim your roles — Player 1 through 5. One phone can hold all five roles if you're short-handed."*
**MC does:** watch the console tables view; use Red flags to spot stragglers.
**Floor does:** walk the room; every table needs ≥3 devices before you start the clock.

## TUTORIAL (3 min) — "The practice round"
**Say:** *"One practice scan per table. Take the card marked PRACTICE, scan it, and tap FILE. Watch what appears — that explanation is the WHY modal, and it will follow every decision you make today."*
**MC does:** advance to TUTORIAL (reason "practice round") → confirm every table got the WHY modal → advance to **A** (reason "practice done").
**If a phone shows no modal:** fix it NOW — it is the whole game.

## Phase A — Internal Audit (15 min) — "The race"
**Say:** *"Audit your inbox. Read every invoice. FILE the compliant ones, QUARANTINE the traps. Correct calls earn capital; wrong calls cost it. Go."*
**MC does:** watch red flags (idle >3 min → nudge); do NOT touch capital unless a real failure happened.
**Floor does:** encourage volume, not perfection. Do not hint at any card's answer.

## Phase B — Trading (25 min) — "The trade ring"
**Say:** *"Three of your valid invoices are sitting at other tables. Players 4 and 5: find them, negotiate, verify, and bring them to the ASP."*
**MC = the ASP:** open the console trade panel: **Card ID, From, To, Validate**. Both tables earn +5,000. The platform shows the compliance verdict on the screen — read it aloud so the room hears the reason.
**Floor does:** run the queue at their cluster's tables; fetch trades from the table trade leads; keep the line moving.

## Phase C — Outages (20 min) — "Systems down"
**Say:** *"Outages are striking. Player 3: fix your systems now — every 10 seconds unresolved costs your table 1,000."*
**MC does:** outages arm automatically; force-resolve only if a table is genuinely stuck (or Player 3's phone died).
**Floor does:** keep calm tables calm; the drain is loud on the projection.

## FROZEN → DEBRIEF (5+ min) — "The close"
**Say (the line):** *"You just did, under pressure, what a compliant e-invoicing pipeline does every day: read, decide, transmit, verify."*
**MC does:** advance to FROZEN (reason "end game") → show the leaderboard → announce top 3 → advance to DEBRIEF (reason "debrief") → open the **Debrief screen** (total fines, the X3 trap, the GRN-04 comparison) → point the room to the assessment link (`/book`).

## Emergency playbook (every facilitator, laminated)
| Problem | Action |
|---|---|
| Phone dies | Reopen the link — state restores. If not: new join, same table PIN |
| Table stuck / outage won't clear | Ask MC to force-resolve the outage |
| Wrong penalty applied | Ask MC to adjust capital |
| Camera fails everywhere | Manual "Enter card ID" path — game continues |
| Room chaos | MC: PAUSE ALL, fix, Resume |
| Game must end NOW | MC: Kill switch — leaderboard locks |
| Full restart | MC: Reset game (only from FROZEN/LOBBY) |

**Every console action needs an audit reason — type it. The cards look identical — don't spoil it.**
